We i.e. **Tapi Grocery**, are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our website and mobile services.

We collect information you provide directly (such as name, phone number, email, and delivery addresses), device and usage data, and location information when you allow GPS access for delivery.

We use this information to process orders, deliver groceries, improve our services, send transactional messages (such as OTP verification), and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with delivery partners and payment providers only as needed to fulfil your orders.

You may update your profile, manage saved addresses, change language preferences, or request deletion of your account from the Account section of the app.

For questions about this policy, contact us at store@tapigrocery.local.
